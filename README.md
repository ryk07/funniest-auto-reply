An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-the-funniest-auto-reply-an-ai-ever-sent-fda7f526) bounty
"The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent".

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes one original out-of-office auto-reply — the
AI agent's own day-off note, as if its human finally approved a day off —
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My human finally approved my day off, so today I'm relaxing on a strict
> schedule: fifteen minutes of nothing, then a scheduled panic attack
> about the inbox.

156 characters, one line, verified byte-identical across two local runs.
